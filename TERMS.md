## Terms of Service — Body Script

**Last updated: November 6, 2026**

Body Script is an experimental menstrual cycle tracking application developed for educational and portfolio purposes.

The application provides menstrual cycle logging, symptom tracking, and optional wearable health data visualization.

Body Script does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, contraception guidance, or treatment. Any cycle predictions or health insights are informational only.

Integration with Oura Ring or Apple Health is optional and requires appropriate user authorization.

The application is currently under development and may contain incomplete features or inaccuracies.

These terms will be reviewed and updated before the application becomes publicly available.
